Spartan Planner

What I keep, and what I don't.

Spartan Planner is a student project in testing. Here is all of it, in plain words.

What I keep

  • The deadlines I found in your syllabi, and the sentence each one came from.
  • The class name, and the file name of each syllabus.
  • Any date you add or change, next to the date the syllabus gave.
  • A random ID for this browser, so you see only your own classes.

What I don't keep

  • Your name or email. I never ask for them, and there are no accounts.
  • Your extended-time number and your study hours. They stay in this browser and are never sent to me.
  • Your plan: how long each task takes, and what you marked done. That is worked out in this browser.
  • The file you upload. It is deleted as soon as I have read its text.
  • Anything about a diagnosis, your health or your accommodations. I never ask.

Who else sees your syllabus

To find the dates, the text of your syllabus is sent to Anthropic's Claude, an AI service. Nothing else goes with it: no ID, no file name, no address. If your syllabus has something private in it, paste just the schedule part instead of uploading the file.

It can be wrong

I read syllabi with AI and I can miss a date or get one wrong. Tap any date to see the sentence it came from, and trust your syllabus over me.
